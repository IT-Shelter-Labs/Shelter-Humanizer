Shelter Humanizer 1.0.0

Extract the entire ZIP, then open Shelter-Humanizer.exe. Python is not required.
The desktop interface is in Russian. English documentation:
https://github.com/IT-Shelter-Labs/Shelter-Humanizer/blob/main/README_EN.md

1. Paste text with Ctrl+V or "Вставить" (Paste), or open a UTF-8 TXT file.
2. "Выбрать ИИ" (Choose AI) -> "Установить локальный ИИ" (Install local AI).
3. Select a model -> "Установить и включить ИИ" (Install and enable AI) -> confirm.
4. Wait for the download. Then "Улучшить текст" (Improve text).
5. Review the result on the right -> "Копировать" (Copy) or "Сохранить TXT".

Default model: Qwen 3.5 4B (qwen3.5:4b), about 3.4 GB; preferably 16 GB RAM.
Portable Ollama adds about 1.4 GB. Saved model choices are preserved.
Alternatives: DeepSeek R1 0528 Qwen3 8B (5.2 GB, preferably 24 GB RAM or a suitable GPU),
the compact May 2025 release, not the newest full DeepSeek series;
Qwen 3 1.7B (1.4 GB, preferably 8 GB RAM, lower quality on complex input).
Qwen 3.5 9B (6.6 GB) is also available. Hardware guidance is approximate.
Start with short text. Reasoning models can be slow or exceed the 180-second timeout.

Downloads start only after confirmation; weights are not included in the EXE.
After installation local editing works without internet. The app's own AI process
closes with the app; an independent Ollama installation is unaffected.
Files are saved to %LOCALAPPDATA%\ShelterHumanizer\ai. Close the app and delete
that folder to remove its downloaded models and runtime.

Alternative: "Обработать в ИИ-чате..." (Process in an AI chat) prepares a task
for your existing chat. Copy it, send it manually, then paste the answer back.
API mode needs your service's base URL, model ID and key; service fees may apply.
The selected service receives the full input and style sample. Keys and text
history are not stored automatically; explicit JSON reports include both texts.

Without AI, only character inspection, cleanup and limited rule-based edits run.
Good text may remain unchanged. "Проверить текст" inspects without rewriting.
"Настройки текста" (Text settings) contains optional controls;
"Проверки и изменения" (Checks and changes) opens diagnostics and comparison.
Light editing, second AI proofreading, special-space replacement and NFC are on
by default. Latin-lookalike replacement is optional and off by default.

Recognized unwanted characters are cleaned AFTER AI editing and proofreading,
including external chat imports. Valid Cyrillic, quotes, dashes, accents and emoji
are preserved. Quotations, URLs and code are protected from automatic cleanup.
Check flagged protected content manually. UTF-8 is an encoding, not an AI marker.

Always review meaning and facts. No plagiarism score, authorship detection or
AI-detector passing guarantee is provided. Light/dark themes are at the top.
Ctrl+V/C/X/A and undo/redo work with Russian and English keyboard layouts.

The build is unsigned. Windows may show SmartScreen. Verify the source and the
SHA256 checksum; do not disable protection.
